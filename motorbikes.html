What We Do

Motorbike repair & maintenance

From a routine service before a weekend ride to a full fault-find, we look after the parts that keep you safe and moving in UAE heat.

Routine Servicing

Oil and filter changes, spark plugs, air filter, coolant and fluid checks to your bike's service schedule.

Brakes

Pads, discs, brake fluid and lines inspected and replaced so you stop when you need to.

Tyres, Fitting & Balancing

Tyre fitting and wheel balancing, with pressures and wear checked for hot road surfaces.

Chain, Sprockets & Drive

Cleaning, adjustment and replacement of chains, sprockets and drive components.

Suspension & Forks

Inspection and repair of forks and rear shocks when the ride feels soft, harsh or unstable.

Electrical & Battery

Battery testing, charging system checks, lighting and wiring faults traced and fixed.

Diagnostics & Fault-Finding

We find the cause before we quote, so you only pay for the work your bike needs.

Pre-Purchase & Pre-Ride Inspections

Buying a used bike or heading out on a long ride? We check it over first.
