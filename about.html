Our Story

Straight talk. Fair prices. No shortcuts.

Western Auto & Marine LLC was built around a simple idea: RAK drivers deserve dealer-level expertise without dealer prices or a long drive to Dubai. With 15+ years of hands-on experience, our technicians diagnose first, quote clearly, and only do the work you actually approve.

The idea was cooked up by real petrol and speed heads — people who've been exploring the deserts and oceans of the UAE for over 40 years, and wanted a workshop that actually understands what that equipment goes through.
